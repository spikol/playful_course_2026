# Classroom Activity Design Sprint

### Day 3 · Your turn

*Everything you have done for two days was someone's design.*
*Today you are the designer.*

**[Design canvas (PDF)](a5_Design_canvas.pdf)** — one per group

---

## The brief

Design an IoT + AI activity **for your own students, in your own subject, in your own room.**

Not for an ideal class. For the class you actually have on a Tuesday afternoon.

By 14:30 it must be **runnable**: another group will play it as your students — including the awkward ones.

---

## Three constraints (they are the creative fuel)

**1 · Cheap** — under 200 DKK in materials beyond the micro:bit
**2 · Safe to fail** — a student who breaks it learns something; nothing is ruined
**3 · Genuinely playful** — a student would choose to keep going after the bell

If your idea fights a constraint, the constraint wins. Redesign.

---

## The design canvas (one per group, A3)

| Field | Forces you to decide |
|---|---|
| **Class + subject** | who exactly is this for? |
| **Core concept** | what is the ONE idea inside it? |
| **What students do** | verbs, not topics |
| **What could go wrong** | name your failure modes now |
| **What it simplifies** | the transposition check, applied to yourself |

The last row is the workshop's whole argument in one cell. **Fill it honestly.**

---

- Everything from Days 1–2 is remixable: the card deck, the blind tasting, sticky-note trees, the storytelling game, the prompt challenge, the sensor bridge
- **Teachable Machine** — image, audio, pose · **ML for Kids** — worksheets

- The Claude bridge with a rewritten system prompt (*"you are a nervous houseplant"* is one line of text away from *"you are a lab safety inspector"*)

Stealing is the method. Cite your sources to each other.

---

## Timeboxes

**09:00 – 09:15** — pick partners, pick a class, pick a concept. Decide fast; the canvas will correct you.
**09:15 – 10:30** — design on the canvas. Build only what you must to test the idea.
**10:30 – 11:00** — standing round: *one minute per group* — what it is, where you're stuck, what you need.

Then the seminar — designed to be useful for exactly where you'll be stuck.

**13:00 – 13:45** — finish a runnable version.
**13:45 – 14:30** — playtest swap.

---

## The standing round: format

One minute. Three sentences:

1. **"We are building ___ for ___."**
2. **"We are stuck on ___."**
3. **"We need ___."**

Peers and facilitators respond with **resources, not solutions.**
Your stuck point is probably shared by two other groups. Say it out loud.
